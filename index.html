Full-Court ASEAN Black Mass Network — Country Evaluation & Module Sizing

Strategy revision: a distributed Cetacea Circular pre-processing footprint across ASEAN + Australia, built on Whalefall modular LiBS lines (1 / 1.5 / 2 / 2.5 t/hr; reference module: ≥1 t/hr, 60 × 12 m, ~850 kW operating, 5 crew, 120–180 day install). All black mass is contracted via offtake to Whalefall's GEN4 hydromet capacity in China, wrapped in Cetacea Carbon / Baiji CDRBM certification (green traceability for China & EU EPR / PCF / Battery Passport compliance), with Phase 3 ECRU / Carbon value-add as a later option. Modules are sized to local feedstock forecasts — e.g. Australia: 2 × 1 t/hr (NW WA mining-swap + NSW/QLD eastern volumes, or SA for BESS).

CDRBM certification architecture

How Certified Decarbonised Recycled Battery Materials evidence flows from each network module, through the Singapore hub, to Whalefall's China hydromet output — supplying China & EU EPR / PCF / Battery Passport compliance. Mass-balance chain of custody per ISO 22095 (site-level, one registry); PCF per ISO 14067. What CDRBM certifies: recycled origin & chain of custody, decarbonised processing (green-power share), and PCF data quality — final market declarations remain the customer's, built on CDRBM data.
